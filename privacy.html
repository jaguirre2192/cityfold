Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

The short version

Cityfold does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The guides, maps and pop-up artwork are built into the app, and it doesn't keep any record of how you use it.

Services from Apple and Wikipedia

When you look up restaurants, cafés or hotels in a guide, Cityfold asks Apple Maps for places near that neighbourhood. That request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Cityfold does not use your location.

"Read on Wikipedia" opens the article in Safari. What happens there is covered by the Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy.

Children

Cityfold is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
