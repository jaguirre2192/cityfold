Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026 · Published by Infinity Machine LLC (Jorge Aguirre)

The short version

Cityfold (published by Infinity Machine LLC / Jorge Aguirre) does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The guides, maps and pop-up artwork are built into the app. Your passport stamps, saved trips and settings are kept on your device only (and in your personal OS device backup if enabled) and are never sent to us.

Location. If you turn on location from My Trip, Cityfold uses it strictly on your device to tell whether you're in one of its cities, find the nearest pop-up and mark your passport "in person". Your location coordinates are never stored on a server, sent to us or shared with third parties. You can turn location permission off at any time in system Settings.

Camera (iOS Tabletop). On iOS, the tabletop book uses the camera to place a guide on a real surface in augmented reality. Nothing the camera sees is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. (The Android app does not request camera access.)

Notifications. The optional City of the Day reminder, audio tour controls and live walking route notifications are generated locally on your device. No remote push service or user account is involved.

Purchases & Audio Packs. Buying All Cities and downloading optional city narration packs are handled by Apple through the App Store (on iOS) or Google through Google Play Billing and Play Asset Delivery (on Android). Infinity Machine LLC never sees or stores your payment details.

External map and reading services

When you look up restaurants, cafés or hotels in a guide, Cityfold queries fixed neighbourhood coordinates (never your personal device location) via Apple Maps on iOS (Apple's privacy policy) or OpenStreetMap Overpass on Android (OSMF privacy policy). Walking directions open in Apple Maps or Google Maps.

On iOS, each city's local weather comes from Apple Weather for the city's centre, never your location.

"Read on Wikipedia" opens the article in your browser (Safari or Android Custom Tabs). What happens there is covered by the Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy.

Children

Cityfold collects no personal information from anyone of any age.
