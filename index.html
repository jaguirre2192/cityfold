Common questions

How do I open a book?

On foldable phones, unfold your phone: the book opens with it. On other phones and tablets, swipe or tap the cover.

How do I get back to the library?

Use the back gesture or tap the back button in the top-left corner.

Why don't I see restaurants or hotels?

Live neighbourhood dining and hotel listings need an internet connection.

Does Cityfold collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
