Common questions

How do I open a book?

On iPhone Duo, unfold your phone: the book opens with it. On other iPhones, swipe or tap the cover.

How do I get back to the library?

Swipe in from the left edge of the screen, or tap the bookshelf button.

Why don't I see restaurants or hotels?

Places in the Paris guide come live from Apple Maps and need an internet connection.

Does Cityfold collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
